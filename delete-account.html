Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Stryde account and all associated data at any time. Here's exactly how, and what happens to your data when you do.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to saseesdk@gmail.com from the email address your Stryde account is registered with.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Stryde account", and include your in-app username so we can find your account quickly.
  3. We'll confirm your identity and process the deletion. Your account and all associated data are permanently removed — this cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Profile (email, username, avatar)Deleted
Daily step historyDeleted
Coins, XP, level, streaksDeleted
Team membershipsDeleted
Claimed map territoryDeleted
Run history and GPS routesDeleted

Nothing is retained after deletion — there is no additional retention period. Deletion cascades through every record tied to your account.

Partial data deletion

Stryde does not currently offer deletion of individual data types without deleting the whole account — requesting deletion removes everything listed above together.
