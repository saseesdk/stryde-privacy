Delete your Stryde account

You can delete your Stryde account and all of its data at any time, straight from the app or by email.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Stryde and go to your Profile.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and all its data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email

  1. Email support@buenoworks.app from the address your Stryde account uses.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Stryde account" and include your username.
  3. We confirm it's you and delete the account, usually within a few days and at most within 30 days.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Profile: email, username, display name, avatarDeleted
Runs, GPS routes and claimed territoryDeleted
Daily step totals (if you turned step tracking on)Deleted
Coins, XP, level, streaks, avatars you unlockedDeleted
Team memberships and challenge entriesDeleted

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. Your runs also disappear from the anonymous community map. If you own a team, ownership passes to its longest-standing member so the team keeps going for everyone else (a team with no other members is deleted).

A Remove ads subscription is billed by Google Play, not by us. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it: cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Deleting only some data

You don't need to delete your whole account to limit what's shared: in Profile → Privacy you can hide yourself from leaderboards and hide your territory and runs from other players. For any other partial request, email support@buenoworks.app.
