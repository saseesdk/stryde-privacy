Stryde

Run it. Own it. Coming soon

What it is

  • Claim your city. Close a loop on a run and the land inside it is yours. Someone else can take it back.
  • Collect coins for every kilometre and every closed loop, and spend them on runner avatars.
  • Climb the ranks on city, team and everyone leaderboards.
  • Replay every run in 3D and share it as a video.
  • Privacy first. The community map shows where people run, not who. The first and last 200 m of every run are cut off, and you can switch your runs off.
