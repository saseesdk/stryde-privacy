Location data

  • The app requests precise (GPS) location only to trace the route of a run you start yourself, so a closed loop can be claimed as territory on the map. Location is never collected in the background outside of an active run.
  • A run's finished path is stored in our database and shown on your own map and, if territory sharing is on, on public or team leaderboards as a claimed shape — never as a live, moving position. Other users never see where you currently are.
  • You can deny location access and still use step-tracking and leaderboard features; only the Map/territory feature requires it.

Health data — what we read, and what we don't

  • We request read access to step counts only via Health Connect (Android) or HealthKit (iOS). We never read heart rate, location, sleep, workouts, or any other health data.
  • Only daily step totals ever leave your device — a single number per day. Raw sensor data, per-minute activity, and step timestamps stay on your phone.
  • Health data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It powers exactly the features you see: your progress ring, coins, streaks, and leaderboards, and nothing else.
  • You can revoke step access at any time in Health Connect or your device's Health settings; the app keeps working with syncing paused.

Advertising

  • The app shows optional rewarded ads (watch an ad, earn coins) via Google AdMob. AdMob and its ad-network partners may collect an advertising identifier and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, and — if you consent via the App Tracking Transparency prompt (iOS) or your device's ad settings (Android) — to personalize them.
  • This data is collected and processed directly by Google/AdMob under Google's own privacy policy, not stored on our servers.
  • Declining tracking permission doesn't stop ads from showing — you'll just see non-personalized ones instead.

What other users see

  • Your username, avatar, level, and period step totals appear on public leaderboards only while "Show me on leaderboards" is enabled in your profile (on by default — you always see your own row regardless).
  • If you join a team, its members can see your username, avatar, and step totals within that team. Leaving the team stops this immediately.
  • Claimed map territory is visible to nearby users as a shape with your username attached; your live, moving location is never shown to anyone.

Data sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Data only flows to the following processors, strictly to run the app:

  • Supabase — our backend provider, hosting the database, authentication, and server-side logic (coin calculations, leaderboards, territory claims).
  • Google AdMob — serves rewarded ads and may collect advertising identifiers as described above, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Health Connect / HealthKit — the operating system's own health data broker; we only read step counts through it, under permissions you control directly in your device settings.

We do not use any other analytics, crash-reporting, or marketing SDKs at this time. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Storage, security & international transfers

Your data is stored with Supabase (hosted Postgres), protected by row-level security: your step and coin records are readable only by you, and all coin awards are computed server-side rather than trusted from the app. The app communicates with the backend exclusively over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Because our backend and ad partners operate infrastructure in multiple countries, your data may be processed outside the country you live in. Wherever it's processed, it remains subject to the protections described in this policy.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists, so your history, streaks, and territory stay intact across sessions. To delete your account and all associated data — profile, steps, coins, team memberships, and claimed territory — contact us at the address below. Deletion cascades through every record and cannot be undone.

Your rights and choices

Regardless of where you live, you can:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate profile information directly in the app (username, avatar) or by contacting us.
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time, as described above.
  • Opt out of public visibility by turning off "Show me on leaderboards" in your profile, without losing access to any other feature.
  • Revoke permissions (location, health data) at any time through your device's settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below — we'll respond as quickly as we can.

Children's privacy

Stryde is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and the "last updated" date above whenever this policy changes materially. We encourage you to review it occasionally.
